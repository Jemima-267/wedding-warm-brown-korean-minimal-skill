# Korean Warm-Brown Minimal Wedding Illustration Skill

[简体中文](README.md) | [English](README.en.md)

This is an AI creative skill for transforming wedding photographs into soft, restrained, and airy Korean-style wedding stationery illustrations. It preserves the source image's essential composition and relationship between the subjects while unifying the linework, reducing unnecessary detail, and creating a refined warm-brown visual style through generous negative space and subtle low-saturation accents.

## Key Features

- Faithfully preserves poses, relative positions, height relationships, and hand interactions
- Retains the defining characteristics of hairstyles, clothing, backgrounds, and floral arrangements
- Uses stable warm-brown linework with nearly consistent stroke weight
- Simplifies facial features into elegant, mature, symbolic forms
- Substantially reduces detail in wedding dresses, suits, backgrounds, and flowers
- Uses cream white and warm ivory as the foundation, with restrained low-saturation accents
- Emphasizes negative space around the couple and in the upper part of the composition
- Avoids photorealistic portraiture, automatic photo tracing, anime styles, and 3D rendering

## Intended Uses

This Skill is suitable for creating:

- Wedding invitation illustrations
- Wedding welcome board illustrations
- Wedding keepsake cards
- Wedding social media artwork
- Korean minimalist wedding stationery visuals

## How to Use

1. Download or copy the repository's `SKILL.md` file.
2. Load its contents into an AI tool that supports image understanding and image generation.
3. Upload a clear wedding photograph as the primary reference image.
4. Ask the AI to transform the photograph according to the Korean warm-brown minimalist wedding illustration Skill.
5. For revisions, use focused requests such as “make the linework more consistent,” “simplify the characters,” “reduce the floral details,” or “add more negative space to the background.”

Full-body or three-quarter-length photographs are recommended. The subjects' poses, hand interactions, clothing silhouettes, and main background structure should be clearly visible.

## Core Visual Principles

The final image should follow these principles:

- **Composition fidelity:** Do not arbitrarily change poses, placement, clothing types, flower varieties, or the main background structure.
- **Consistent linework:** Use smooth, rounded, clean, medium-fine warm-brown lines; avoid heavy black contours and sketch-like strokes.
- **Minimal characters:** Simplify facial features while retaining natural adult proportions and an elegant appearance.
- **Reduced clothing detail:** Preserve recognizable silhouettes while removing most folds, lace, embroidery, and fabric texture.
- **Restrained background:** Retain the essential structure while removing distracting details so the background supports the couple.
- **Faithful florals:** Simplify the original flowers rather than replacing them with unrelated species or redesigning the arrangement.
- **Negative-space priority:** Use cream white, warm ivory, and warm-brown line art as the foundation, with only a small amount of muted color.

## Output Goal

The final result should look like a professionally designed Korean wedding invitation illustration—not a photo filter, automatic outline, or realistic portrait. Its overall character should be:

> Soft, clean, romantic, restrained, airy, modern, and elegant.

## Notes

- This repository provides creative rules and prompts; it does not include an image-generation model or standalone application.
- Results depend on the quality of the reference photograph and the image-editing capabilities of the selected AI model.
- Make sure you have permission to use the reference photographs, and respect the privacy and portrait rights of the people shown.
- Before commercial use, review the terms of service and content license of the AI platform you use.
